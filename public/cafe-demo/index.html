01The space

An old market floor,
left mostly as we found it.

We kept the terrazzo, the arched windows and the exposed brick. Everything else is quiet: two long oak tables, forty-two chairs that don’t match, and enough light to read by.

The counter faces the street so the room gets the evening. Music is vinyl on Fridays and Saturdays, and low enough to talk over the rest of the week.
