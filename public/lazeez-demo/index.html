[ The card ]

The menu, autumn 2026.

The card is short on purpose. We cook what the valley gives us — haakh and morels in season, mutton from the high pastures, and a tandoor that runs from noon to close.

Prices in INR · all meats halal · card changes with the season

01

Biryani

aged basmati, sealed handis, slow dum

  1. Dum Pukht Mutton Biryani Signature

    ₹680

    Kashmiri mutton and aged basmati sealed under dough, finished on a slow dum for four hours.

  2. Hyderabadi Chicken Biryani

    ₹560

    Saffron rice, browned onion, and mirchi ka salan on the side.

  3. Murgh Malai Biryani

    ₹590

    Cream-marinated chicken, green cardamom and fried shallot.

  4. Subz Zaffrani Biryani

    ₹460

    Seasonal vegetables, saffron and whole spice, sealed the same way.

  5. Yakhni Pulao

    ₹520

    Lamb stock rice with fennel, green cardamom and slivered almond.

Served with raita and a spoon of Kashmiri pickle.

Kashmiri & Indian

wazwan roots, cooked the long way

  1. Rogan Josh Signature

    ₹720

    Mutton in Kashmiri chilli and ratanjot, cooked without onion or garlic.

  2. Butter Chicken

    ₹580

    Clay-oven chicken, tomato, fenugreek and a little cream.

  3. Nadru Yakhni

    ₹480

    Lotus stem in a yoghurt gravy with fennel and mint.

  4. Paneer Tikka

    ₹420

    Hung-curd marinade, ajwain, charred on the skewer.

  5. Dal Makhani

    ₹360

    Black urad simmered overnight with butter and smoked cream.

Rice, haakh and saffron naan are ordered alongside.
