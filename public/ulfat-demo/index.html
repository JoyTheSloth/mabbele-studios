01 / The spirit of the house

Not simply a place.
A feeling, held
in time.

Ulfat speaks of affection. Khas, of something cherished. Together, they name an imagined house where hospitality is personal, beauty is patient, and nothing asks you to hurry.

Here, the graceful language of Mughal courtyards meets Kashmiri-inspired chinar and paisley details. Woven textures, drawing loosely on the diverse craft landscapes of Northeast India, bring an earthy counterpoint to royal formality.

Not a recreation of a single tradition. A considered conversation between them.

For the love of lingering.
